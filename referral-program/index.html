The Referral Program

One messaging system for eight dealership referral offers

I rebuilt three generations of referral messaging into a configurable system for Vinsyt. The work covers the offer model, conditional copy, channel variants, and the path from invitation to sharing and redemption.

Role

Offer modeling, message architecture, UX writing, share and redemption flows, and event configuration.

Team
Me and a lead developer.
Surfaces
Email, SMS, app notifications, landing pages, and QR-based redemption.
Stage
In production. The message examples are design specimens.

The offer model

Model the offer before writing the message

Dealerships can reward the owner, the friend, both, or neither, across sales and service. The copy has to describe that configuration accurately without making a reward appear where none exists.

The configurator makes those choices visible beside their output. I used it to connect the commercial settings to the language a customer would actually receive.

010203040506

Message architecture

Assemble the message from the offer

Instead of maintaining a complete copy set for every case, I built one structure for each moment. Conditional blocks determine which reward lines appear; tokens supply the dealership, salesperson, vehicle, and amounts.

The side-by-side variants show why this matters. The invitation remains recognizable, but its promise changes with the offer. Blocks that do not apply are left out.

The recipient

Write for the person receiving it

The owner already knows the dealership. The friend may not. Their first message needs to explain who is contacting them, why, and what has been offered before asking them to act.

The no-reward case needs a different emphasis too. Without an incentive to lead with, the invitation rests on the relationship with the salesperson rather than implying a benefit that is not there.

The share path

Keep sharing deliberate

The design includes two routes: the owner shares a personal link, or supplies a chosen contact for an introduction.

010203040506

The message sequence

Design the sequence as well as the individual send

The send plan connects the invitation to a completed visit or vehicle handover, then defines the channel timing and cancellation checks. Once sharing has happened, the remaining invitation sequence should stop.

I wrote the email, SMS, and app variants as parts of that sequence rather than unrelated messages. Each needs enough context for its channel without repeating the whole program.

Beyond the inbox

Carry the offer beyond the inbox

The landing and reward pages continue the same offer into the next step. The QR destination connects that digital path to redemption with an advisor at the counter.

The customer should not have to reinterpret the reward as they move from a message to a page to an in-person interaction.

The shared delivery infrastructure is covered in the Lifecycle Engine. This project focuses on the offer, the language it generates, and the path the customer follows.
